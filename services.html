FAQ
Does $250 include a domain and hosting?

No. Domain registration and hosting are separate so the business can own and control those accounts directly.

Do I own the finished website?

Yes. The goal is to hand you a finished site you can keep using, subject to any third-party services or assets you choose to use.

Can the price change?

$250 is the starting price for a straightforward small-business site. Extra features, unusually large sites or special integrations may cost more, and that would be discussed before work begins.

Do I have to choose one of the concepts?

No. The concepts are only examples. You can bring your own idea, reference a style you like, or let Redbud create an original direction for your business from scratch.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. Every site is designed and tested for mobile screens as part of the build.
